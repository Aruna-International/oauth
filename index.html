666 Seconds — Channel Uploader

A private publishing tool for my own YouTube channels

This page describes the application that asks for access to a YouTube account during Google sign-in. It is not a public product and it has no users other than me: it is my own tool, which I use to publish my own documentary videos to my own channels.

What the application does

  • Uploads my finished video files to my own YouTube channels.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, category and privacy status.
  • Uploads caption (subtitle) tracks and a custom thumbnail image.
  • Reads back the resulting video identifiers so it can record what it has already published.

Channels

I publish documentary videos about history — forgotten kingdoms, institutions, companies and ideas — on two channels I own and run myself, one in English and one in Arabic.

Access and permissions

The application uses Google's OAuth 2.0 flow. It requests only the YouTube scopes it needs to upload and manage my own videos, and I am the only person who grants it access. It never posts to, reads from, or modifies any account other than mine — the one that explicitly grants access at the Google consent screen.
